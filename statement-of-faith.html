Statement of Faith

“The Worship of God should be spiritual; therefore we seek to remain flexible and yielded to the Holy Spirit to direct our worship.”
  1. We believe that there is one living and true GOD, eternally existing in three persons: The Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, equal in power and glory; that this triune God created all, upholds all, and governs all things. (Genesis 1:1; Deuteronomy 6:4; Isaiah 44:8 and 48:16; Matthew 28:19-20; John 10:30; Hebrews 1:3).
  2. We believe that the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the final authority for faith and practice.
  3. We believe that salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by works, lest any man should boast.
  4. We believe in the deity of Christ, His virgin birth, His sinless life, His miracles, His vicarious and atoning death, His bodily resurrection, and His imminent return.
  5. We believe the Holy Spirit is a person who convicts the world of sin, regenerates sinners, and empowers believers for witness and service.
  6. We believe in the spiritual unity of all believers in Christ and that the local church is the primary expression of that fellowship.
  7. We believe that the Lord Jesus Christ instituted two ordinances for the church: (a) full immersion water baptism of believers, and (b) the Lord's Supper. (Matthew 28:19; Luke 22:19-20; Acts 2:38; 1 Corinthians 11:23-26) We also believe that the Lord Jesus Christ validated the ordinance of marriage. (Matthew 19:4-5 and John 2:1-11)

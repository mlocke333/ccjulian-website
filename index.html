Who We Are

Calvary Chapel Julian is a fellowship of believers under the Lordship of Jesus Christ. Our greatest desire is to know Him and to be conformed into His image by the power of the Holy Spirit.

We believe the true basis of Christian fellowship is God's love, which is greater than our differences.

In our services we believe the following:

  • ✦ Spiritual Worship — We seek to remain flexible and yielded to the Holy Spirit to direct our worship.
  • 🎵 Inspirational Worship — We give great place to music in our worship.
  • 📖 Intelligent Worship — We emphasize Bible teaching so God may instruct us as to how He would be worshiped.
  • ❤️ Fruitful Worship — We look for His love in our lives as the greatest evidence that we are truly worshiping Him.

Statement of Faith

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the final authority for faith and practice.

"The Worship of God should be spiritual; therefore we seek to remain flexible and yielded to the Holy Spirit to direct our worship."

We believe in the one true God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

We believe that salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone — not by works, lest any man should boast.

We believe in the deity of Christ, His virgin birth, His sinless life, His miracles, His vicarious and atoning death, His bodily resurrection, and His imminent return.

We believe the Holy Spirit is a person who convicts the world of sin, regenerates sinners, and empowers believers for witness and service.

We believe in the spiritual unity of all believers in Christ and that the local church is the primary expression of that fellowship.

For a complete statement of our foundational beliefs, we follow the Calvary Chapel network's statement of faith rooted in the authority and sufficiency of Scripture.

Our Servants & Leadership

Meet the people who serve and lead our church family

Our church is led by a team of pastors and servants who are called and committed to shepherd the flock of God. We operate under the authority of Scripture and the leadership of the Holy Spirit.

Calvary Chapel Julian is part of the Calvary Chapel fellowship — a network of independent, Bible-believing churches that emphasize verse-by-verse teaching of God's Word.

Core Values of Our Leadership

  • 📖 Bible-Centered — We are committed to the Authority and inerrancy of Scripture as our sole guide.
  • 💧 Spirit-Led — We rely on the Holy Spirit to direct our ministry and empower our service.
  • ❤️ Love-Driven — We lead with humility, compassion, and sacrificial love for those we serve.
  • 🌍 Outreach-Focused — We are committed to reaching the lost and making disciples of all nations.
